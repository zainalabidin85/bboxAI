Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Your images stay on your computer

bboxai-desktop stores your projects, images, labels and trained models on your own computer. When you use bboxai-remote, requests pass through our relay server to reach your desktop. The relay forwards them but does not keep copies of your images or models.

What the hosted service stores

  • Account and device: your bboxAI username and an ID for your paired desktop. Your password is checked by your own desktop, not stored on our server.
  • Wallet: a record of token purchases, spending and unlocked features.
  • Payments: order records (amount, status and Billplz bill ID). Card and bank details are handled by Billplz and never reach us.
  • AI Assist quality data: whether suggested boxes were accepted, edited or deleted, with their box coordinates and the IDs of the example images used. This helps us choose better examples for your project. It does not include image content.
  • Terms acceptance: when you agreed to the Terms & Conditions.

AI Assist and third parties

When you click AI Assist, the image you're labeling and a few of your already-labeled images are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate suggestions. They are processed under Anthropic's commercial terms and are not stored by bboxAI. Payments are processed by Billplz. Videos on our landing page are hosted by YouTube and load only when you press play.

Your choices

You can use bboxai-desktop entirely offline, without the hosted service. To ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted, email admin@unitani.com. Records we must keep for payments may be retained as required by law.
