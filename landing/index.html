How it works

bboxAI runs on your own computer. Remote access is an optional extra layer on top.

1

Install bboxai-desktop

Download the installer for Windows or Linux and create your account. Remote access switches on automatically.

2

Label your images

Create a project, add classes, and annotate photos or video frames on your desktop or phone.

3

Train and use your model

Start training with one click. When it finishes, check the metrics and download best.pt.
