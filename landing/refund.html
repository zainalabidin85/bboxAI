Refund Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Token purchases

Tokens are digital credits that are added to your bboxAI account as soon as your payment is confirmed. Because of this, tokens are non-refundable once purchased, except where required by law.

When we will help

Contact us and we will investigate and correct the problem, either by crediting the missing tokens or refunding the payment, if:

  • you were charged but the tokens never appeared in your wallet,
  • you were charged more than once for the same purchase, or
  • a paid feature failed because of a fault on our side and tokens were deducted anyway.

Failed AI Assist calls are never charged, and unlocking a detailed report or live camera test for a training run you already unlocked does not charge you again.

How to request help

Email admin@unitani.com within 14 days of the payment with your bboxAI username, the payment date and amount, and the Billplz bill ID or receipt if you have it. We aim to reply within 3 working days. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method through Billplz. Processing time depends on your bank.
